How to use

1. Click a blue node when you already know it → it turns green and unlocks what depends on it. 2. Grey nodes are blocked until every prerequisite is green. 3. Click a grey node to see its missing prerequisites and choose to mark them known or set a goal.

Kahn's algorithm

In-degree counts the blocking prerequisites not yet processed. Nodes with in-degree 0 are queued; each pop lowers the in-degree of its dependants.

Model vs provisional gold

Gold = edges labelled by Claude (not human verified). green = accepted edge present in gold, orange = accepted edge not in gold, red dotted = gold edge the model missed.
