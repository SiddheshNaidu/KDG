# Evaluation plan (accreditation evidence)

All targets are PROPOSED values, "to be confirmed after census and the first golden run". No target promises 100%. Baselines quoted are measured facts from `docs/evidence/model_v5_facts.txt`.

## E1 Parser
- Dataset: golden courses in `tests/golden/<family>/` (5 per family where the family has that many; status DRAFT until a human signs off).
- Labeller: PROPOSED = one faculty member signs off each golden course; two signers on a 20% sample to measure agreement. Needs professor confirmation.
- Metrics per family: topic precision, recall, F1 (exact normalised match, plus a looser token-overlap match reported separately); junk rate = share of emitted topics violating validity rules R1-R8; module-count agreement; course-detection recall (courses found / courses listed in the PDF's own course table).
- Report format: `reports/parser_<family>.csv` + a table in the evaluation report; failing courses listed by code.
- PROPOSED targets: junk rate <= 2%; topic F1 >= 0.90 per family on golden; a family below target is marked PARTIAL in its doc and gated (FR-003) rather than hidden. Justification: the audit (`reference/audit_report.md`) counted 234 malformed and 151 over-dropped topics in the old extraction, so an order-of-magnitude reduction is the improvement to prove.

## E2 Edges
- Dataset: human gold set, >= 300 pairs from courses held out by course group (reference: v5 test had 721 rows from the teacher table, `model_v5_facts.txt`), stratified: same-module / cross-module, model-accepted / uncertain / rejected, across at least 3 families.
- Labeller: PROPOSED = two faculty independent labels, disagreements adjudicated; report Cohen's kappa. Needs professor confirmation (who, how many hours).
- Metrics: precision, recall, F1, PR-AUC at the served threshold; calibration curve and expected calibration error on held-out data; precision of the `accepted` band; share of pairs in the `uncertain` band.
- Comparisons (all on the same gold set): (a) position-only baseline, (b) v4 and v5 artifacts from `reference/` (v5 test numbers on the teacher table: F1 0.8000, PR-AUC 0.8577; v4: F1 0.4135, PR-AUC 0.4758), (c) new model with and without position features, (d) with and without each structural rule.
- PROPOSED targets: precision of `accepted` >= 0.85 on gold with recall reported alongside; ECE <= 0.08; the new model beats the position-only baseline on PR-AUC by a margin measured, not assumed.
- Known risk to report: teacher labels are chain-biased (mean parents per child 1.143; 38.4% of confident edges adjacent-only; `model_v5_facts.txt`). The report states this limitation next to the numbers.

## E3 Graph sanity
Per Course: is DAG (must be 100%), in/out-degree distribution, share of Edges that are adjacent-only, share of Topics with no parent and no child, number of transitive-reduction removals. PROPOSED: DAG 100%, no more than a stated share of orphan Topics (value fixed after first run).

## E4 UI end-to-end
Playwright suite for FR-040..FR-047 and FR-060..FR-066, run in CI; screenshots stored as evidence. PROPOSED: 100% of P0 specs pass.

## E5 Security
Automated checks SEC-001..SEC-011 run in CI; `pip-audit` / `npm audit` reports archived; a manual review checklist signed by the owner covering SEC-012 and SEC-013.

## E6 Performance
Measure cold start, per-course and per-PDF latency with `scripts/bench.py` on the demo machine; targets fixed from the measurement (D4).

## Reporting
`reports/evaluation_report.md` generated by one script from CSVs; every number links to its CSV; failures listed, not averaged away.
